Your route through BIOS

A clear guide to choosing a mode, forming a team, completing screening and reaching the hackathon.

01 · About

What BIOS is

BIOS (Bringing Innovation On-Stage) is the hackathon of the CSED Department at Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, run with the IEEE Student Branch. Teams take an idea from a first sketch to a working prototype, on site on 30, 31 October and 1 November 2026.

SEED, CORE and FRONTIER use a Screening round before the hackathon. Open Innovation and Research follow their own presentation schedule.

02 · Process

How participation works

  1. 01

    Create your BIOS account

    Sign in, complete your profile and provide the required participant information.

  2. 02

    Choose your participation

    Each team participates in one main mode: SEED, CORE or FRONTIER, with selected FRONTIER teams able to move exclusively into invitation-only FORGE. CORE and FRONTIER participants can also enter Open Innovation, Research Track, or both as independent add-ons.

  3. 03

    Follow your track

    CORE and FRONTIER teams choose and submit only for their own mode. A team that accepts FORGE leaves FRONTIER and works and submits only on its assigned FORGE briefs. Open Innovation and Research teams prepare their own idea or research.

  4. 04

    Present your work

    Shortlisted teams move through later rounds. Open Innovation presents on 31 October; Research Track presents on 1 November.

03 · Participation

Who each mode is for

SEED

SEED

School students working on SDG-focused ideas with support from an approved school SPOC.

CORE

CORE

First-time and emerging builders looking for a structured, accessible challenge.

FRONTIER

FRONTIER

Experienced teams ready for a more demanding technical and product challenge.

FORGE

FORGE

Invited teams that move from FRONTIER into the separate FORGE mode and work only on assigned industry briefs.

There is no direct FORGE registration. Accepting an invitation moves the entire team exclusively into FORGE.

Independent add-ons

Open Innovation and Research Track

Enter either add-on—or both—even if you already belong to a CORE or FRONTIER team. Your add-on entry does not change that team or its submission. New participants may enter directly. SEED participants are not eligible.

Add-on · Open Innovation

Bring your own idea

Newcomers work on a problem of their choice, with mentorship on 30 October and final evaluation on 31 October. Startups with an idea or pitch deck may present to professionals and alumni and skip mentorship if they do not need it.

Enter Open Innovation

Add-on · Research Track

Ask a better question

New to research? Start with a guided problem statement and mentoring on 30 October. If you already have a topic or do not need mentoring, bring your own work directly to the final presentations on 1 November. Individuals and teams are welcome; teams of four are recommended for beginners.

Enter Research Track

Already submitted for CORE or FRONTIER?

You can still enter either add-on yourself. Complete its separate registration, then participate individually or create/join a team of up to four for that add-on. Your CORE or FRONTIER team, mode and screening submission stay unchanged. A person can participate in both add-ons, with a different team or solo entry in each.

04 · Registration

Registering and joining

Only a team’s leader completes the full registration form. Everyone else joins with the team code, and every participant verifies their identity and accepts the policies.

SEED

Schools and their SPOC

  1. A teacher or staff member registers the school as its SPOC (single point of contact). The organisers review the request.
  2. Once approved, the SPOC shares the school code with students.
  3. Each student registers with the school code, a school-issued ID and a guardian consent form signed by a parent or guardian.
  4. The SPOC verifies each student, and verified students form their teams.
SEED registration

CORE and FRONTIER

The team leader registers

The leader provides their institution, enrolment number, phone number and college-issued ID, then accepts the policies. They create a team on the dashboard and share its code. A team keeps one mode.

Team code

Joining a team

For CORE or FRONTIER, teammates join with the team code on their dashboard. For an add-on, each person registers for that add-on first, then uses its separate team code. Add-on teams have at most four members and do not alter CORE or FRONTIER membership. Every participant verifies their identity and accepts the policies.

Verification

Identity and consent

  • College and university participants upload a college-issued identity card.
  • SEED students upload a school-issued identity card and signed guardian consent, and their SPOC verifies them.
  • Every participant accepts the Privacy Policy and the Participation Terms.

Find teammates by skills

  1. Open Find teams by skills from your dashboard. If you cannot recruit, use the Requests and Messages history links instead.
  2. Solo participants may contact other solo participants or the leader of a team with room. Only the leader may contact a solo participant on behalf of a team. Teams do not contact other teams through matching.
  3. A request opens a one-to-one chat when accepted. It does not reserve a place or add anyone to a team.
  4. To join, enter the team code on your dashboard. The normal capacity and mode rules apply. For Screening teams, joining and leaving close when the team submits or Screening ends. Earlier chats remain readable.

07 · Results

Shortlisting and later rounds

How shortlisting works

For SEED, CORE and FRONTIER, the organisers review each screening submission and mark the team shortlisted or not selected. Shortlisted teams continue to the later rounds. Open Innovation and Research have their own presentation schedule.

Where you see the result

Your dashboard shows your team’s result for each round, whether it can continue and what to do next, as soon as the organisers publish it. Keep an eye on the announcements as well.

08 · Community

Campus ambassadors

Refer friends to BIOS and climb your campus leaderboard. Only verified CORE and FRONTIER registrations count.

Selected participants near the top of each campus leaderboard will become campus ambassadors.

How referrals count

  1. Share the referral link or code from your dashboard. You can share it before registering for a mode.
  2. Your friend enters the code during their first CORE or FRONTIER registration. A code entered after their first registration cannot be added later.
  3. The referral stays pending until you submit a CORE or FRONTIER registration and your friend’s CORE or FRONTIER registration is approved. These steps can happen in either order.
  4. Once both conditions are met, the referral counts toward your points and campus leaderboard position.

A code used during your friend’s first registration stays linked while it is pending. If either condition is never met, it does not count.

09 · Support

Mentors and help

Mentors

Mentors are experienced people who help teams with direction and feedback. Teams browse the mentor directory and can keep up to 3 requests pending at a time. When a mentor accepts, they appear on the team’s dashboard.

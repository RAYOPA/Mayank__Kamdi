01 The idea

BIO plus OS

In a computer, the BIOS is the first thing that runs. Here it is the first point of contact, where life and computation begin speaking to each other.

BIO the intelligence that grows

Biology, humanity, nature, emotion and culture. These systems adapt, heal, remember and surprise. They evolved over millions of years without being designed.

OS the intelligence that is built

Operating systems, algorithms, machines and engineering. These systems are specified, scheduled and run. They are designed line by line, then set loose.

Neither is enough on its own. BIO + OS = BIOS: a hackathon where the two build together.

02 BIO

What grows

BIO is the living half of BIOS: cells and forests, people and the communities they make.

  • Living systems

    Bodies, ecosystems and oceans: systems that heal, adapt and keep their balance without a manual.

  • People

    Health, learning, language and culture shape the needs and habits behind every good idea.

  • A shared planet

    Climate, water, food and energy: the problems that belong to everyone, and to the Sustainable Development Goals.

On the BIO side, every project begins with a question: who is this for, and what should grow?

02 OS

What is built

OS is the engineered half of BIOS: systems that turn an idea into something specified, scheduled, tested and shipped.

  • Systems

    Operating systems, networks and infrastructure: the layers everything else quietly stands on.

  • Algorithms

    Data, models and AI support learning, prediction and decisions at scale.

  • Machines

    Devices, robots and secure software: code that meets the physical world, and has to be trusted there.

On the OS side, every project ends with an answer: make it run, make it hold, and put it on stage.

Mentors

Interested in mentoring BIOS?

Mentors help teams unlock roadblocks, refine strategies, and ship better prototypes. Here’s what we expect:

Minimum Requirements

  • Solid track record in tech, product, research, or entrepreneurship.
  • Availability for at least two syncs during the build sprint.
  • Comfortable guiding teams asynchronously on Discord or email.

Responsibilities

  • Review team direction and offer tactical feedback.
  • Share resources, frameworks, or connections that accelerate execution.
  • Keep organisers informed if any assigned team requires extra support.

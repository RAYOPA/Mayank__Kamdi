Participation Terms

Rules that apply when you register, join a team or submit work.

Version 2026-10-04

Eligibility and accurate information

Participants must meet the eligibility rules for their selected mode and provide accurate registration information. Organisers may reject or revoke a registration when information is false, incomplete or cannot be verified.

Mode and team

Participants may change between CORE and FRONTIER while teamless during Screening, provided registration for the destination mode is open. Joining a CORE or FRONTIER team in the other mode changes the participant's base mode after confirmation. That mode and team membership become final when the team submits; base-mode changes also close when Screening ends. Open Innovation and Research Track are independent add-ons: eligible participants may enter either or both, solo or on a separate team of up to four, without changing a CORE or FRONTIER team. SEED participants cannot enter these add-ons. Every participant must complete their own verification and acceptance. SEED and FORGE otherwise follow their separate eligibility rules.

Submissions

Teams must submit their own work before the stated deadline and follow the required format. A team may update a submission only when the round permits resubmission. Organisers may remove unsafe, unlawful, copied or misleading material.

Judging and shortlisting

Organisers and appointed judges assess submissions using the announced process. Shortlisting decisions appear on the participant dashboard. Decisions may change if eligibility or integrity checks identify a problem.

Project ownership and display

Participants retain ownership of their work. By submitting, the team permits BIOS to review the material and display approved project information for event administration, judging and the public project gallery.

SEED and FORGE

SEED students require school verification and signed guardian consent. FORGE is a separate, invitation-only mode. When a leader accepts a FORGE invitation, the whole team leaves FRONTIER, participates and submits only in FORGE, and follows any additional rules set by its assigned industry partners.

Event changes

Organisers may update schedules, venues or operational rules when necessary. Material changes will be announced through the platform or official BIOS channels.

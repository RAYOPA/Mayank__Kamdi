A separate, invitation-only mode for teams working exclusively on assigned industry briefs.

Open Innovation

Build or pitch an idea you bring to BIOS.

For newcomers

Bring any idea of your own. Develop it with mentors on 30 October, then present on 31 October.

For startups

Pitch an idea or deck to professionals and alumni. Join the mentorship session only if you need it.

Research Track Open

Research Track

Explore a research question, new or ongoing.

For beginners

Start with a guided problem statement and mentoring on 30 October.

For researchers

Bring your own topic or existing work and present your findings on 1 November.

  • Participate solo or in a separate team of up to four
  • Final presentations 1 November
